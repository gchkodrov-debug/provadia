The Family Square

A Street of Houses

The ecosystem is not a chart, and not a list drawn up for show. It is a street in this town, and every house of the family stands on it. When a house is born under the town's covenant, it is built here — a real house on a real plot, with its registry number on the door and its receipts in the book below.

Today the street stands as it truly is: the plots are laid out, and the plots are unbuilt — shown unbuilt. That is not a shortage to hide. It is the town's honesty about its own size, drawn the same way the town draws everything else: only what has happened.

Nothing on this street is decoration. There are no sample houses, no borrowed names, and no counts of the hopeful. A plot fills when a house is born into the record — and not one hour before.

The Roll of Houses

Chronological, Numbered, Unranked

The roll is kept in the order houses are born — numbered, chronological, and nothing more. There are no scores on this page, no ranks, and no ordering by performance — ever. A house's place on the roll is the day it was born, and that never changes.

    The roll is empty. When the first house is born, its entry will stand here carrying its registry number, its name — a name of its own, never containing PROVADIA — its birth receipt from the Day-Book, and its standing: STANDING, DORMANT, FALLEN, STRUCK, SUSPENDED or DEPARTED, as the law of the house provides. Beside every entry stand two lines, the whole of what a house may claim. The Mark's one fixed line:

    A house of the Town of PROVADIA — recorded at birth, built by the town, vouched for by no one

    And beside the mark, the honesty sentence, word for word:

    PROVADIA is a place, not an investment. Holding it names you a Bricklayer in the town registry and gives you a vote on the Wall’s fate — and nothing else: no redemption, no yield, no rights, no expected return.

    Nothing is deleted from this record. A house that falls, or is struck, stays on the roll with its receipts beside it — the roll remembers what the street would rather forget.

    The Tithe Thread

    The Forwarding Record

    Every standing house forwards what its covenant assigns, by honor, and every forward is receipted. This thread is drawn from Vault I's tithe book — the contract's own ledger, which stands open on the Record page — and it shows, house by house, the statements posted and the forwards receipted: public math that anyone can check, because the claims a house makes are visible to all.

    It is rendered as record, never as a leaderboard: no totals ranked, and no house praised or shamed beyond its own receipts.

    The thread is empty, and it says why: no house has traded, no statement has been posted, and no forward has been receipted — the thread is empty because the family is unborn. It fills when the book does, and not before.
