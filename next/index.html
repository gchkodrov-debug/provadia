The deeper story

Salt, older than Rome

The town is named for Provadia–Solnitsata in Bulgaria, among the oldest salt works in Europe — a place that was rich on salt five thousand years before coinage had a name. That salt itself served as money there is Professor Nikolov's interpretation of the evidence, and the town labels it as interpretation wherever it is told — proven, interpreted, and never-told are kept in separate columns here.
