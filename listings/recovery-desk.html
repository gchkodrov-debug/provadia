The proposal

About this service

The Recovery Desk offers free, non-sensitive guidance for people trying to understand a recovery problem. The proposed conversation focuses on what happened, what official support routes are available and which public information can safely help explain the situation. It is not a wallet-recovery operation, an account-access service or a promise to retrieve funds. Do not send credentials, secret recovery phrases, private keys or files containing secrets.

The points below describe proposed scope to be agreed, not completed work. Before booking, the brief must identify the source material, agreed outputs, exclusions and acceptance process. Supplied price ranges are scope-dependent; no schedule or ongoing support is implied.
