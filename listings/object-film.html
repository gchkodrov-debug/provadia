The proposal

About this service

Object Film proposes a short visual treatment centred on an approved object, artwork or product asset. The project begins with a storyboard and an agreed viewing context, so pacing and output format follow the intended use rather than an arbitrary feature checklist. The film may require supplied photography, source models or approved original artwork. Catalogue illustrations are not proof that the final film or the object itself has been produced.

The points below describe proposed scope to be agreed, not completed work. Before booking, the brief must identify the source material, agreed outputs, exclusions and acceptance process. Supplied price ranges are scope-dependent; no schedule or ongoing support is implied.
