The proposal

About this planned product

This proposed preparation kit would organise the relationship between token-related assets, names and metadata fields. Its purpose would be to reduce ambiguity in a handoff, not to assert that metadata is correct, permanent or already published. The project would need to supply the intended schema, approved material and destination requirements. Validation rules and export behaviour would be designed around that agreed scope rather than assumed from the product title.

This is a planned product offer, not implemented or tested software and not a ready-to-download asset. Before work could be commissioned, the brief would need to define inputs, exact outputs, rights, dependencies and acceptance criteria. The price remains blocked until the captain supplies the source schedule; there is no purchase or fulfilment flow.
