The proposal

About this door

The shelf holds what it holds — this door is for everything it doesn’t. If you know what you want and can describe it — the specs, the purpose, what “done” looks like — send it through this door. We read it, we answer honestly whether it is something we can build, and if it is, you receive a written quote covering the product and the services around it: the build itself, plus any setup, wiring, or media the work needs.

Nothing is charged before you approve that quote and its scope in writing. The build then runs under the Completion Contract: the acceptance checks are agreed before work begins, the files and control are handed over as the work proceeds, and the delivery is receipted in the public record. If your spec is outside what we can build, the answer is a plain no — with the reason — and no charge.

Boundaries & prerequisites

We build only what we can build. A spec outside our craft gets a plain no, not a charge. No work begins before the written quote is approved. Custom builds keep the house fences: nothing that promises returns, nothing that moves markets, nothing that imitates another project’s identity.

Payment follows the house rail: the quote is in dollars, you pay by the route agreed in it, and the conversion is recorded in the Day-Book. The 80% founder / 20% website & community split applies to custom work as to every service.
