The proposal

About this service

Launch Identity Bundle proposes a coherent visual direction for the material surrounding an announcement. It is intended for founders who want the website, launch communication and supporting assets to feel related rather than assembled independently. The starting point is an approved brief and permission to use the supplied assets. The work does not invent missing PROVADIA coin faces, a mask or any other canonical identity artwork.

The points below describe proposed scope to be agreed, not completed work. Before booking, the brief must identify the source material, agreed outputs, exclusions and acceptance process. Supplied price ranges are scope-dependent; no schedule or ongoing support is implied.
