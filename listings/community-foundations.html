The proposal

About this service

Community Foundations proposes the written and structural starting point for a community space. The work concerns how people arrive, where different conversations belong and what happens when a problem needs attention. It avoids treating channel creation as evidence of a functioning community. Member growth, round-the-clock moderation and ongoing operations are not implied by this planning engagement and should only be discussed as separately defined responsibilities.

The points below describe proposed scope to be agreed, not completed work. Before booking, the brief must identify the source material, agreed outputs, exclusions and acceptance process. Supplied price ranges are scope-dependent; no schedule or ongoing support is implied.
