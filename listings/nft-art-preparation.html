The proposal

About this service

NFT Art Preparation proposes a practical workflow for organising approved artwork and its accompanying metadata. It starts with the files and rights the founder actually has, then plans naming, relationships and export requirements for an agreed collection. This is preparation work, not a representation that a collection has been minted, listed or sold. No price was supplied for this service, so a scope quote is required before a booking can be considered.

The points below describe proposed scope to be agreed, not completed work. Before booking, the brief must identify the source material, agreed outputs, exclusions and acceptance process. Supplied price ranges are scope-dependent; no schedule or ongoing support is implied.
