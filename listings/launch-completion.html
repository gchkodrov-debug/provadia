The proposal

About this service

Launch Completion is presented here as the Launch Studio tier interpretation requested for this catalogue. Each tier is a proposed delivery structure, not a sourced promise of a fixed feature count. The work begins by examining the founder’s existing material, then records what can reasonably be completed within an agreed engagement. A selected tier must be confirmed in writing before booking; neither the tier name nor the price establishes that a launch has been audited or approved.

The points below describe proposed scope to be agreed, not completed work. Before booking, the brief must identify the source material, agreed outputs, exclusions and acceptance process. Supplied price ranges are scope-dependent; no schedule or ongoing support is implied.
