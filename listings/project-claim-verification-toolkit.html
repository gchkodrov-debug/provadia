The proposal

About this planned product

This proposed toolkit would help a project or reviewer organise claims alongside the references that might support them. The workflow would separate the wording of a claim, the evidence provided and the limits of the conclusion a reviewer can draw. It would not automate truth or promise that every assertion can be verified. Review roles, evidence handling and decision vocabulary would be agreed before a working toolkit could be delivered.

This is a planned product offer, not implemented or tested software and not a ready-to-download asset. Before work could be commissioned, the brief would need to define inputs, exact outputs, rights, dependencies and acceptance criteria. The price remains blocked until the captain supplies the source schedule; there is no purchase or fulfilment flow.
