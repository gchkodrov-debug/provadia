The proposal

About this service

Founder Website proposes a focused web presence that introduces the founder, explains the project and gives visitors an honest next step. The engagement brings content structure, visual direction and responsive presentation together around material the founder can substantiate. It is not a promise of a live hosted service, backend integration or continuous maintenance. Those operational responsibilities need their own written agreement rather than being hidden inside a design price.

The points below describe proposed scope to be agreed, not completed work. Before booking, the brief must identify the source material, agreed outputs, exclusions and acceptance process. Supplied price ranges are scope-dependent; no schedule or ongoing support is implied.
