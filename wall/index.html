01 / 04 · The 20% Flow

The 20% Flow

A permanent 20% allocation of the coin's creator fees dedicated forever to building the Wall. It channels real trading-volume proceeds straight toward physical gold acquisitions without touching holder wallets.

02 / 04 · The 1-Gram Bullion Standard

The 1-Gram Bullion Standard

Physical 1-gram serial-numbered gold bars placed into allocated storage at a named licensed third-party vault the founder never holds. Each brick lights on the Wall only after its purchase invoice and vault receipt are logged in public.

03 / 04 · The 47-Brick Threshold

The 47-Brick Threshold

The monument's fixed finish line: 47 physical bricks or the coin's first birthday on November 18, 2027, whichever comes first. Reaching either mark closes accumulation and triggers a public livestream followed by a Bricklayer vote on the Wall's fate.

04 / 04 · The Honesty Boundary

The Honesty Boundary

The permanent legal and architectural fence around the monument: the gold is never backing for the coin and is never redeemable. Holding PROVADIA names you a Bricklayer with a vote on the Wall's fate—and nothing else.
