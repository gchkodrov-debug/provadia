The Chamber of Trust

The Twin Scales & the Sealed Codex

Four commitments stand over the full text of the town's law. The text beneath them is the law itself — all of it, in order, readable by anyone. Nothing is summarized away, and nothing hides behind the pictures.

01 / 04

THE NON-INVESTMENT BOUNDARY

PROVADIA is a place, not an investment. Holding it names you a Bricklayer in the town registry and gives you a vote on the Wall's fate — and nothing else: no redemption, no yield, no rights, no expected return.

02 / 04

DATA MINIMIZATION & PRIVACY SHIELD

An account stores only what it needs to work: an email address, a salted hash in place of your password, a display name if you choose one, a wallet address if you choose to connect one. The town never holds your keys, your seed phrase, or custody of anything — and nothing from a private conversation is ever printed in the public record. Genesis serials are drawn unlinked from membership and carry no personal information on-chain.

03 / 04

THE NO-LOSS & COMPLETION LAW

Services are charged at completion only — completion means a handover test the customer can check unaided — with a thirty-day support window and narrow, deliverable-based refund rules printed before any money moves. The no-loss side of that law is the house's own discipline — the house does not lose money on a build — and neither side is asked to trust the other's word, because the receipt is the proof.

04 / 04

CONDUCT & SPEECH PROTECTION

Criticism, disagreement, and hard questions — of the project, of the Captain, of the founder — are protected speech in every room of this town, always. Profanity and abuse draw one calm warning; a second offense is removal, logged in the open — and money paid through a returnable rail is returned through that rail.

Privacy · The Account

What a Member Account Stores

A member account stores only what the account needs to work: your email address, a protected scramble (a salted hash) of your password — never the password itself — a display name if you choose one, and a wallet address if you choose to connect one. That is the whole list.

Looking at this town needs no account at all. An account exists so the Keeper can keep your conversation, so your name can stand in the registry when you join it, and so the town can tell one Bricklayer from another.

Privacy · The Chamber

Talking to the Keeper

When you type to the Keeper, your words travel to the town's own server to be answered, and the Keeper's answer comes back as text and as a voice note made by our server, in the town's own voice.

If you press the microphone, your speech is turned into text by your own device and its maker (for example Apple or Google) — that audio never comes to us. We receive only the text you press send on.

A member's conversations are kept with the member's account, so the member can return to them. A guest's single question is answered and is not kept as an account record. Separately, the Keeper keeps an anonymous tally of what visitors ask about — themes and counts only, never names, never words — so the town knows what its guests are looking for. That tally is the Book of Wants, and it belongs to no one person.

Privacy · Doors and Records

Support Mail and the Public Record

Mail you send to support@provadiacoin.com is kept so we can answer it and keep the record of the answer. We never DM first, and no one from this town will ever ask for your seed phrase, your keys, or a "claim" link — anyone who does is not us.

The town's public record — the Day-Book and the registry — prints only what the town's law prints: a registry name is written when a member joins the registry, and a ban is logged by handle, date, and reason under the conduct law. Nothing from a private conversation is ever printed in the public record.

This site sets no advertising trackers and runs no analytics that follow you from place to place. The only storage your browser keeps for us is what signing in requires.

Privacy · The Line

What We Never Do — and Your Choices

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for marketing. We do not build profiles of visitors. If the law ever compels a disclosure, it will be the narrow one the law compels, and no wider.

Your information is yours: write to support@provadiacoin.com and ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete your account. Deletion removes the account and its private conversations. If your name stands in the public registry and you want it struck from the roll's face, write to us and it will be struck.

This town is not directed at children, and accounts are for adults.
