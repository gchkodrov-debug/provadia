Terms · The Place

What This Place Is

PROVADIA is a place, not an investment. Holding it names you a Bricklayer in the town registry and gives you a vote on the Wall's fate — and nothing else: no redemption, no yield, no rights, no expected return.

Nothing on this site, and nothing the Keeper says, is financial or investment advice. The coin trades on open markets the town does not control; its price can fall, and it can go to zero. What you do with your own money is your own decision, made with your own judgment.

Terms · The Keeper

The Keeper and the Record

The Keeper answers from the town's public record, and he speaks of nothing outside it — he does not speak of price, and he never asks for keys. Like any keeper, he can be wrong. The record itself is the authority: every claim in this town carries its receipt, and the Verifier on the Record page lets you check the chain yourself, in your own browser.

One claim, one receipt — or it never happened. Where the record and any words on this site disagree, the record governs.

Terms · Accounts and Conduct

Accounts and Conduct

A member account is free. You are responsible for your own credentials, and for your own wallet: the town never holds your keys, never asks for your seed phrase, and can never recover a wallet for you. Guard them as your own.

One law of conduct covers every room of this town. Profanity and abuse earn one calm warning; a second offense is removal. Criticism, disagreement, and hard questions — of the project, of the Captain, of the founder — are protected speech here, always, and are never an offense. Every ban is logged in the Day-Book by handle, date, and reason.

On a ban, money paid to the project through a returnable rail — a payment the project itself took and can send back, such as a card payment for the town's own work — is returned through that same rail. Market trades cannot be un-traded, and the coin stays the holder's own property: a ban closes the community, never a man's wallet.

Terms · The Limits

The Town's Limits

The town is provided as it stands. Its doors open when they are real and close when they must; no page of this site promises a schedule, a feature, or a return. The Golden Wall belongs to no one: its gold is never backing, never redeemable, and is sold to no one — its covenant stands on the Record page, exactly as written there.

Questions of these terms go to support@provadiacoin.com — answered by a person, on a standing watch.
