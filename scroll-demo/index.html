The Studio · How It Is Made

Two Stills, One Assembly, Your Scroll

The craft begins with two pictures of the piece: one whole, one taken apart. The motion between them is built frame by frame — two hundred and forty stills on this page — and your scroll is the projector. Scroll down, the work comes together; scroll up, it returns to its pieces. Nothing plays without you.

The mask on this page is the Keeper's own, the ancient face that stands on the reverse of the town's coin. The subject changes with every product; the standard does not. When the catalogue's presentations open, each one is made this way — for that product, from that product, under the same Completion Contract as everything else the Studio sells.
