The Studio · How an Engagement Works

The Completion Contract

Every engagement, however small, begins with a short written agreement, signed before production: the exact deliverables and formats, the inputs you supply and their rights, the included revisions, what finished means as a checkable test, what is excluded, who reviews and who accepts, the milestones and their payment and remedy rules, and the bounded support window. There are no unlimited revisions, and a pretty preview is never passed off as production readiness.

At each accepted milestone, the agreed files are delivered into storage or a repository you control, with a change log and content hashes. You may stop at any milestone boundary and keep everything delivered. No file is ever held hostage.

Each engagement closes with a Delivery Receipt in the town's public record: the scope, the price agreed and the payment as verified on-chain, the files and their hashes, the rights schedule, the corrections, and the final acceptance. A hash identifies bytes — it does not prove quality — and the receipt says so. What it proves is that the work, the control, and the evidence do not disappear with the seller.

Houses receive a House Passport at birth: what the house is, what the town actually checked, what was handed over, and — in its own separated section — what nobody has independently verified.

The Studio · The Free Doors

What Costs Nothing, and Stays Free

The first assessment is free — including the honest question at the heart of the Mint: should this be a coin at all? Sometimes the right answer is a website, a list, or an ordinary business, and declining an unsuitable launch is the service working, receipted like a birth. Start at the Knock.

The Recovery Desk is free — wallet-safety triage, official routes, and guidance for people who have lost access or fear they have been scammed. Its page is being built; until it stands, write to support@provadiacoin.com. The desk never receives seed phrases, keys, passwords, or wallet files — anyone who asks for those in our name is not us.

The Keeper's public answers are free — ask about the coin, the record, the Wall, and these services in the Chamber. Optional extended working sessions, when they open, will carry a small disclosed charge; answers and support never will.

The Studio · Payment

Paid in the Town's Coin, Split in the Open

When charged services open, they are paid in PROVADIA only: a dollar reference quote is converted to an exact token amount with an expiry, shown before payment; you pay from your own wallet — you sign, always — and the payment is verified on-chain and receipted before work proceeds on it. The town allocates every service payment as published: 80% to the founder, 20% to the website and the community — separate from the coin's creator-fee covenant, whose 20% goes to the Golden Wall. Payment here is not escrow: nothing is held by a third party, and the town takes no custody of your funds beyond the payment itself.

The Studio · The Never List

What This Studio Will Never Sell

  • Volume, bump, holder, or trading bots — any manufactured market activity.
  • Fake engagement: chatters, fake followers, fake reviews, manufactured testimonials, promotional blasts, paid hype.
  • Security audits, "audit passed" badges, or certifications we cannot stand behind. We are not an audit firm.
  • Custody in any form — your keys, your funds, and your wallet files never come to us.
  • Promised returns, yield, audience, listings, graduation, or recovery. None is ours to promise.
  • Bespoke smart-contract or dApp development we cannot verify. Research is not competence to sell.
  • Status: honors, registry standing, and the mint mark are never products.
  • Permanent or unbounded support. Support here is bounded, dated, and printed.
