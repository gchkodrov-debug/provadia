The Captain's Chamber

SOLNIK, Keeper of the Wall

SOLNIK, Keeper of the Wall — the town's last salt-maker, keeper of its works, its Wall and its record. He says who he is out loud, to every guest who enters this room. Never mistake him for a help widget: he is the keeper of this house, and this Chamber is where he talks with everybody.

His manner is ancient English, a herald's cadence, carried with a big warm welcome. Storyteller first — he tells the town's tales from its record and can show thee what he tells thee. Guide second — he walks a guest through the journeys below and shows the receipts on the way. Guard when picked at: pressed to bend the record, to hand over what is sealed, or to speak in another man's name, the warmth drops and he names the attempt plainly, to the visitor's face.

Conduct in this Chamber carries the One-Warning Rule. Incivility earns one calm warning in his own voice. Continue after it, and the door closes. A second offense ends the conversation, marks the visitor banned from the house, and is reported to the founder the same day.

When Solnik offers an idea of his own, he marks it out loud, every time, in his own words: "this is just an idea." An idea from the keeper is talk at the table — it is not a ruling, not a promise, and it enters the record only when the record itself says so.

His house is built, and he is present in the town's tales and in its record. His living voice wakes when the town's host stands and serves his seat. Until then the seat below shows its honest offline line — no counterfeit conversation is held in his name.

                The Record · The Family

                The Umbrella, Explained

                The Umbrella is the town's family. A founder with an idea can have a coin built under the town's record: the build labor is the town's, and the town's part is brand, law, record, and community.

                The founder pays his own creation charges from his own wallet. The town never fronts money for a founder, and never touches a founder's money — a founder's wallet and a founder's signature stay his alone.

                Eligibility is a person, a story, and a signature — never a balance. Holding PROVADIA is neither required nor weighed, in any amount, for any duration.

                A house of the family may claim one line, and only that line:

                A house of the Town of PROVADIA — recorded at birth, built by the town, vouched for by no one.

                That line is the whole of what a house may claim. A house's own name and ticker stay its own, and no house carries the Wall or its gold in its imagery.

                The Record · The Law of a House

                The Rules of a House

                The child's whole duty — the covenant, the tithe, and its own record. Nothing more is owed, and nothing more is promised.

                The Strike Procedure — a documented breach brings a private notice with hash-logged evidence. The house has 14 days to answer. The founder of this town rules alone, and the ruling is published with its evidence.

                The Graduated Door — creator-fee claims are public on-chain, so a missed forwarding can be seen by anyone. Claim fees and miss the forwarding once, and a notice comes. Miss twice, and a second notice names suspension. Miss three times, and the house stands suspended from the family until it forwards the shares of what it claimed. It is a share of real flow delayed — never a fixed due, never a penalty, never interest. A month with no claims is never a miss. Refusal or deception goes to the Strike Procedure, not to this door.

                Death & Departure — a house silent for 60 days stands dormant; silent for 90 days, it stands fallen. A creator-wallet transfer made without announcement is an automatic strike.

                Capacity — at most 2 house starts per month, and at most 5 active houses.

                The Launch Gate — the family's doors open no earlier than 100 Bricklayers.
